## Architecture Guardrails
- `Game` is the authoritative state machine; strategies propose moves and must not bypass collision handling.
- Dynamic cycle updates must be candidate/validate/commit transactions. A rejected candidate must leave the committed state unchanged.
- Grid and cycle rollback are one logical transaction; never restore only one side.
- Treat Hamiltonian cycles as non-unique. Canonical equality is not a substitute for validity checks.
- Keep correctness gates deterministic. Wall-clock limits may gate fixed-hardware benchmarks, not shared CI.
